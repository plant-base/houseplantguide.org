## Introduction

There are many choices to make when it comes to selecting soil for your plant. There are different soil characteristics
that you should take into account that should match the preferences of the plant. Additionally, soil amendments can be
added to the soil. 

## Soil characteristics

When choosing soil for your plant, these 4 characteristics come into play.

- **nutrient richness**: plants need nutrients for growth
- **drainage/aeration**: sufficient drainage prevents root rot and allows the roots to breathe 
- **moisture retention**: moisture needs to be sufficiently retained to allow the roots to take it in
- **acidity**: certain plants prefer more/less acidic soil (lower or higher pH level respectively)

## Amendments

A soil amendment is any material added to soil to improve its physical properties, such as water
retention, permeability, water infiltration, drainage,
aeration and structure.

### **Coarse sand**

Description: Larger grained sand particles.
Characteristics: Provides weight and structure, aids in drainage.

### **Sand**

Description: Finer grained sand particles.
Characteristics: Helps with aeration, drainage, and particle separation in soil.
low water retention

### **Loam**

Description: Balanced soil texture containing sand, silt, and clay.
Characteristics: Ideal soil texture, provides good drainage and moisture retention.

### **Shredded leaves**

Description: Decomposed organic matter in shredded form.
Characteristics: Adds organic matter, nutrients, and improves soil structure.

### **Perlite**

Description: Lightweight volcanic glass with high porosity.
Characteristics: Lightweight, porous, improves aeration and drainage in soil mixes.
Perlite aids with aeration, but has low water retention qualities.

### **Vermiculite**

Description: Mica-based mineral.
Characteristics: Improves drainage, retains water and nutrients in soil mixes.
Vermiculite aids with aeration and has high water retention qualities

### **Pumice**

Description: Porous volcanic rock.
Characteristics: Provides excellent aeration and drainage, lightweight.

### **Peat**

Description: Partially decomposed sphagnum moss.
Characteristics: Retains moisture, improves soil structure, slightly acidic to lower pH
Very high moisture retention, adds a tiny bit of draingage
Also provides moisutre transporation due to capillary effect since it is a fibrous material

### **Limestone**

Description: Mineral used to raise pH in acidic soils.
Characteristics: Raises pH, provides calcium and magnesium.

### **Sphagnum moss**

Description: High-quality moss with excellent moisture retention.
Characteristics: Excellent moisture retention, aids in aeration and root development.
Also provides moisutre transporation due to capillary effect

### **Coconut coir**

Description: Fibrous material from coconut husks.
Characteristics: Retains moisture, eco-friendly, provides good aeration.

### **Bark/wood chips**

Description: Bark from fir/pine trees, used in potting mixes.
Characteristics: Provides aeration and drainage, commonly used in mixes.
High aeration and can hold some moisture

### **Charcoal**

Description: Charred organic matter.
Characteristics: Absorbs impurities, improves soil drainage and aeration.

### **LECA (Lightweight Expanded Clay Aggregate)**

Description: Lightweight porous clay pellets.
Characteristics: Lightweight, porous, provides aeration and retains moisture.

### **Coconut chips**

Description: Larger coconut husk pieces.
Characteristics: Provides aeration, retains moisture, sustainable alternative to bark.

### **Compost**

Description: Decayed organic matter.
Characteristics: Adds nutrients, improves soil structure, promotes microbial activity.

### *Worm castings**

Description: Feces produced by earthworms.
Characteristics: Rich in nutrients, enhances soil structure, boosts microbial life.

### **Lava Rock**

Can be used as a top layer for cactii
very permeable, good drainage
