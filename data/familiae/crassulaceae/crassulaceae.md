---
name: Crassulaceae
common_names: stonecrop family or orpine family.
etymology: The family Crassulaceae is named after the <i>Crassula</i> genus. The name Crassula comes from the diminutive of the Latin adjective <i>crassus</i> meaning <i>thick/dense</i>. Crassula then means <i>a little dense</i>.
